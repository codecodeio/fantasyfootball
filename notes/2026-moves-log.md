## Did the opponent drive these decisions?

Two different "opponents", and they mattered very differently.

**The fantasy opponent (Team 01) changed the *threshold*, not the *picks*.** The matchup projected
102.73 vs 100.77 before any changes, and that 1.96-point margin is why marginal upgrades were worth
making at all. But no player was chosen *because of* Team 01 — we didn't chase a ceiling or shade
to a safe floor, and every move would have been the same against any opponent.

**The NFL defences mostly *corroborated* rather than caused.** The Goedert and Jones calls were made
on projection plus week 1 usage, before I had the points-allowed data; I picked those ranks up
afterwards from the add/drop page.

| Player | Faces | Rank vs that position | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aaron Jones (started) | @ CHI | 7th **most** to RBs | ✅ Confirms |
| MarShawn Lloyd (benched) | @ NYJ | 2nd **fewest** to RBs | ✅ Confirms |
| Romeo Doubs (dropped) | vs PIT | **Fewest** to WRs | ✅ Confirms |
| Harrison Butker (started) | vs IND | 4th **most** to Ks | ✅ Confirms |
| Lions (locked, −2.00) | @ BUF | **Fewest** to DEFs | ❌ Worst spot, unfixable |
| **Goedert (started)** | **@ TEN** | **12th most to TEs** | ⚠️ **Argues against** |
| **Fannin (benched)** | **@ TB** | **4th most to TEs** | ⚠️ **Argues against** |

**Jones-over-Lloyd is the best-supported move** — 2nd-fewest against 7th-most is as clean a split as
the data offers, pointing the same way as the usage.

**The TE swap went against the matchup data, deliberately.** Fannin has the friendlier draw; role
and offence quality were judged to outweigh it, and Rotowire still projects Goedert higher despite
the worse defence. **If Fannin outscores Goedert this week, examine this decision first.**

No WR upgrade existed regardless: Pickens (WAS, 14th fewest), McMillan (ATL, 8th fewest) and bench
option Pierce (@ KC, 2nd fewest) all drew unfriendly defences.
