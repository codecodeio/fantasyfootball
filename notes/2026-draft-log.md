## Hypotheses to score at season's end

1. **H1 — ADP sequencing wins points.** Taking the player whose ADP won't survive to your next pick
   (used at 21, 45, 69, 93, 100) beats taking the highest projection available.
2. **H2 — Wait on QB, except for a tier break.** Mid-tier QB early is wrong (Hurts at 52); a QB who
   is a tier alone and has slid is right (Lamar at 52). Score both halves.
3. **H3 — Yahoo season projections are well calibrated.** Compare per-player proj vs actual.
   Watch for systematic bias by position and by ADP range.
4. **H4 — Bye diversity on the bench pays.** Bench players were deliberately ranked by bye spread,
   not raw projection (Pierce demoted, Reed demoted). Did the covered weeks actually get covered?
5. **H5 — Handcuffing an elite, injury-prone RB1 beats a higher-projected bench body.**
   Black (54.3) over R.White (133.7). Week 1 already gave a signal — see below.
